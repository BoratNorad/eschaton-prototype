HOW TO PLAY // 60-SECOND BRIEFING

  1. It starts small. A few crises are live, and every coalition is in at least one. ⚠ markers are TRIGSITs (triggering situations). At first you can only deploy troops and negotiate at your own crises.
  2. The game ends when no TRIGSIT is left active: each is settled by negotiation, won militarily, or frozen when its timer runs out. But decisions spill over: defeats, nukes, betrayals and huge mobilizations ignite new crises.
  3. Win with the best INDDIR / SUFDDIR ratio: damage you inflict directly ÷ damage you suffer directly. Hit hard, get hit little.
  4. Each turn, everyone secretly queues orders. Then all orders resolve at once. You get 3 actions per turn (strike, spy, deploy, negotiate), plus unlimited builds you can afford.
  5. Resources: IND (industry), URA (uranium) and INF (influence) come from your coalition's territories. Damaged land produces less.
  6. Escalation unlocks danger. Fighting, spillover and nukes raise the meter, and the THREAT LEVEL ratchets up: spies, then tactical nukes on troops, then homeland strikes, then strategic warheads. Everyone starts at their historic peak arsenal, just locked. At 10 it's ESCHATON: every silo fires and the game ends.
  7. Spies reveal an enemy coalition's arsenal, or plant false intel so enemies see fake numbers. Trust nothing.
  8. Treaties: propose a non-aggression pact. Breaking one (or nuking a partner) is announced to the world.

Tip: click a TRIGSIT to deploy divisions or negotiate. Click an enemy country to strike it. Then hit SUBMIT ORDERS.
